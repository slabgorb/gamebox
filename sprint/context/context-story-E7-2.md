## Title
Bots roll their own dice (drop the human proxy-roll)

## Problem
First Clue playtest friction: the human must click 'Roll for {bot}' to resolve a bot's movement roll. Today bots emit a values-less clue_roll_request SSE that any human resolves on their behalf (backgammon proxy pattern; src/clients/clue/ClueApp.tsx rollReq/liveRollReq ~lines 85-146 and the 'Roll for {seat}' button ~lines 197-206). Make bots roll their own die automatically so no human action is needed. CRUX to settle in design: resolve the bot roll server-side inside the orchestrator turn (invisible, no animation — matches the existing collapsed-mechanic doctrine that invisible dice inline server-side), or auto-trigger the DiceTray on a spectator client without the manual button if a visible bot die is wanted. Server-side auto-resolve is simplest and removes the click. Touches the orchestrator bot-turn flow (must still drive the whole turn in one wake-up, gated on activeUserId===bot per the turn-continuation contract) and removes the proxy-roll UI. Leave the human's own visible roll unchanged.
