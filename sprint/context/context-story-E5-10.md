## Title
Conquest advance: attacker chooses armies to move (min=dice rolled, max=armies-1), not all-in

## Problem
Playtest rules bug: on conquering a territory the game moves ALL attacking armies into the captured territory. Correct Risk rule: the attacker CHOOSES how many armies to advance — minimum = the number of dice rolled in the winning (final) attack, maximum = the attacking (origin) territory's armies minus 1 (must leave >=1 behind). Restores the strategic choice of how much to commit vs. hold the origin.

Scope: (1) Server conquest apply accepts an advanceCount and validates it into [dice_of_winning_roll, origin_armies - 1], moving that many instead of all; carry the winning roll's dice count through to the conquest step. (2) Client: after a conquest, present an advance chooser (slider/stepper over the valid range) and send the chosen count — slot into the E5-1 interactive attack overlay. (3) Bots must choose an advance count so bot conquests still resolve in one turn (default: advance max = origin-1, i.e. current all-in behavior, or a simple heuristic). Edge case: when origin_armies-1 == dice (e.g. 2 armies, 1 die) the range collapses to a single forced value — no chooser needed. TDD: assert min/max boundaries and rejection of out-of-range counts before wiring UI.
